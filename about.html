Who We Are

Urban Shield Pvt Ltd is a trusted workforce solutions company specializing in providing trained, reliable, and disciplined manpower for cleaning and facility management services. We work closely with businesses, industries, offices, and commercial establishments to maintain clean, safe, and well-organized environments.

Our workforce is carefully selected and professionally trained to deliver high standards of hygiene, efficiency, and responsibility. We ensure that every individual we provide meets the expectations of modern workplaces and contributes to smooth day-to-day operations.

Understanding that each client has unique requirements, we offer flexible and scalable manpower solutions tailored to different industries. Whether it is routine cleaning or large-scale facility support, we ensure consistent quality and reliability.

At Urban Shield, we don’t just supply manpower — we build long-term partnerships based on trust, professionalism, and performance.
